About us

Global Passport Series emerged from families who wanted more agency over their own agenda.

GPS is not an investment banking or commercial summit. It is designed by families for families — for their inner circle of advisors, business associates and friends — to discuss the global agenda in business, policy, leadership, philanthropy and culture.

Mission

Build exceptional business, philanthropic, leadership and cultural ties across borders and political divides.

Vision

Mix leadership, philanthropic and entrepreneurial spirits to foster a trusted community of affinity.

Authentic

By invitation only, hosted and anchored by families for families.

Themes

Technology, deeptech and AI, energy transition, health and wellbeing, geopolitics, leadership, philanthropy.

Cultural

Local, regional and global engagements in arts, sport and culture.

Delegations

Participation in Davos, FII, COP, UNGA, Art Basel and regional visits.

Munich 2026

Three days of closed-door sessions, dinners and Oktoberfest.

GPS promotes regional and global trade, diplomacy, welfare and cultural exchange. Over three days we evaluate the roles and ambitions we hold for ourselves, our businesses and society — and engage with some of the most pressing opportunities facing the world today.

Who we are

A founder, and a small team who produce every edition by hand.

“Build exceptional ties across borders and political divides.”

John is Chief Business Officer of PMB Capital, a London-based merchant bank and private equity fund founded in 2013 as part of Edmond de Rothschild and later spun out independently. He has 25 years across private equity, operating and corporate finance roles for growth equity businesses, having previously invested at Health Enterprise Partners and DLJ Merchant Banking (Credit Suisse), served as CFO of Time Link International Corp., and begun his career as an M&A analyst at UBS Warburg.

He sits on the boards of Firemind, Global Passport Series, Texas Gulf Bank, the Blaffer Museum, Culturunners and the Autotelic Foundation, and has produced several global art initiatives and documentary films, including the Antarctic Biennale and Culturunners Healing Arts. He holds a BA from Southern Methodist University and an MBA from Columbia Business School.
